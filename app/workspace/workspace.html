What the numbers say

✧

Each weight is a holding’s current value divided by the portfolio total. Effective holdings = 1 ÷ the sum of squared holding weights. Equal weights give a value equal to the number of holdings. This checks allocation concentration only; it does not measure underlying fund overlap, volatility, returns or suitability.

Understand the assumption

We convert your effective annual return to a monthly rate and solve for equal monthly contributions. Existing savings compound for the full period. Contributions arrive at each month’s end. Returns are held constant for this illustration; actual markets fluctuate. Taxes, fees and inflation are not deducted. Results are rounded for display.
