Overview
The 4-Phase Build at a Glance
Each phase proves one specific thing before spending more time/money on the next layer. Sub-engine counts and AI capability scale up deliberately — never build Phase 3 tech before Phase 1 is validated.
STAGE 1 MVP — Minimum Buyable Product Month 0-2.5  |  2-3 people  |  Rs.3-4.5L
Why "Buyable" and not just "Viable": The goal isn't a demo people politely tolerate — it's the smallest version of Nxance a real stranger will actually pay for. If nobody pays at MVP stage (even a small amount), the product doesn't yet solve a real enough problem, and no amount of Beta/Phase 1 polish will fix that.
One job: Prove that AI-translated portfolio insights, from a genuinely useful subset of the Health Check Engine, make someone reach for their wallet.
✅

PaymentEven a token Rs.49-99 one-time "unlock full report" payment — the whole point of MVP is proving willingness to pay, so payment must exist from Day 1, not be deferred.

The Golden Rule Across All 4 Phases: Never build the next phase's technology before the current phase's target metric is hit. Building Rust/C++/CUDA engines before MVP validates willingness-to-pay wastes engineering time on a product nobody's paid for. Building real quantum hardware access before Phase 1's 10,000 users wastes crores on optimisation nobody will notice at small scale. Each phase earns the right to build the next.
Why "Minimum Buyable" matters as the name: Calling it "Viable" lets a team convince itself that positive feedback or high engagement is enough to proceed. Calling it "Buyable" forces the only test that actually predicts a real business: did a stranger pay real money for it, with their own card, without being asked to as a favour.
