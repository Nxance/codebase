Nxance — Technical Brief
What we're building, in plain engineering terms. Give this to any developer, CTO candidate, or technical advisor before the first conversation.
⏰ 10-minute read
Nxance is an AI-powered investment intelligence app for India. It has exactly three core systems: a Health Check Engine that analyses an existing portfolio across stocks, mutual funds, FDs, commodities, and currency to find hidden losses; a Construction Engine that builds a new optimised portfolio from scratch; and NxanceLM, our own AI model, which sits between both engines and the user — turning raw numbers into plain-language advice, and also working as a standalone chat assistant.

The Core Product
The Three Engines — What Each One Actually Does
📊
1. Health Check Engine — 11 Sub-Engines
Analyses an existing portfolio, finds what's costing the user money
What it doesUser connects/uploads their portfolio → 11 specialised checks run (Performance, Cost Leak, Goal Alignment, Fraud Gate, Structure, Behaviour, Tax Efficiency, Forecasting, Liquidity, ESG/Suitability, Peer Comparison) → outputs a Health Score + prioritised issue list.
Hardest partFraud Gate (7 hard rule checks + ML anomaly detection) and Forecasting (GPU-accelerated Monte Carlo, 10,000 simulations).
StackPython orchestrates, Rust does the heavy math (XIRR/Sharpe/correlation, 10-50× faster than Python), CUDA runs the Monte Carlo.
🏗
2. Construction Engine — 14 Sub-Engines
Builds a new, optimised portfolio from the user's goal
What it doesUser states a goal ("Rs.50L in 10 years") → engine profiles risk, allocates across asset classes, filters 7,400+ instruments, runs both classical (MVO) and quantum-inspired (QAOA) optimisation, stress-tests against 5 historical crashes, and outputs an exact SIP plan.
Hardest partQAOA quantum-classical hybrid optimisation — runs on GPU-simulated quantum circuits today, with a path to real quantum hardware later.
StackC++ (Eigen) for the optimisation math, CUDA for QAOA, Rust for fast filtering/backtesting across the full instrument universe.
🧠
3. NxanceLM — Our Own AI
Not a separate feature — woven through both engines, plus standalone chat
Role 1Inside Health Check — translates 11 sub-engines' raw numbers into plain-English issue cards, cross-references overlapping findings.
Role 2Inside Construction — turns vague user goals into structured inputs, asks clarifying questions, explains why a portfolio was chosen.
Role 3Standalone chat — general finance Q&A, grounded in live market data and the user's own portfolio context.
ArchitectureHybrid Transformer + Mamba (State Space Model) + Mixture-of-Experts — same pattern as NVIDIA Nemotron 3 and AI21 Jamba. Attention layers for precision, Mamba layers for long-context (full portfolio history), MoE for domain specialisation without inference cost blowup.
One model, three jobs. This is why the architecture decision (hybrid, not plain decoder-only) matters — it needs precision AND long-context AND conversational fluency all at once.
