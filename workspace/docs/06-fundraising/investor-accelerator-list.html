Outreach Strategy
How to Sequence This List
Week 1-2: Apply to non-dilutive schemes first (#87-97) — zero cost, zero dilution, no downside. Start with HP-specific ones (#93-95) since approval odds are highest there.
Week 2-4: Submit to equity-free/low-equity accelerators (#51, #60-63 — Google, AWS, NVIDIA, Microsoft) for cloud/AI compute credits, which directly reduces NxanceLM's training cost.
Week 3-6: Warm-intro outreach to Category A funds (#1-36) — these are the highest-probability cheque-writers for Nxance's exact stage. Prioritise #4, #11, #17, #21, #33, #34, #35 for direct fintech/India/AI thesis overlap.
Ongoing: Apply to 2-3 accelerator cohorts (#49, #50, #68, #69) in parallel — even rejections generate investor relationships, and acceptance brings both capital and a credible signal for the rest of the list.
