Master Architecture
NxanceLM — Complete System Diagram
NxanceLM sits at the centre of Nxance. It receives structured data from quantitative engines and converts it to plain language. It also handles direct user questions about finance, economy, markets, and portfolios.
4 Operating Modes
NxanceLM — Mode-by-Mode Architecture
NxanceLM automatically detects what the user needs and routes to the correct mode. Each mode has its own processing pipeline and output format.
📊
Mode 1 — Health Check Explainer
Receives structured output from E-01 through E-07. Converts Health Score, issue data, and Selection Engine picks into a clear narrative. Explains why each issue costs money, what the exact Rs. impact is, and what the step-by-step fix is. Always includes before/after Health Score projection.
🏗
Mode 2 — Construction Explainer
Receives portfolio construction output. Explains why each instrument was selected (FIT SCORE, key reasons), what the asset allocation means for the user's specific goal, how QAOA optimized the weights, and what the Monte Carlo probability is. Shows exact Rs. per instrument and SIP plan.
🗣
Mode 3 — Financial Intelligence Chat
Handles any finance question directly. Economy, GDP, inflation, sector analysis, policy, RBI decisions, global markets, country comparisons, startup ecosystem, IPO analysis, company fundamentals, government schemes. Pulls live data from APIs. Answers in plain language with India context always.
⚖
Mode 4 — Portfolio Comparison
User provides two portfolios (or compares their portfolio vs a benchmark). NxanceLM runs both through E-01 analysis modules, generates a side-by-side comparison of XIRR, Sharpe, TER, goal alignment, overlap, and Health Score. Picks winner per category with exact Rs. difference.

Sample Chat Conversations
RBI ne aaj interest rate 0.25% cut kiya. Mere portfolio pe kya asar hoga?
NXANCELM — RBI RATE CUT ANALYSIS RBI ne Repo Rate 6.25% se 6.00% kar diya aaj. Yeh teesri consecutive cut hai 2025 mein. Aapke portfolio pe 3 direct effects honge:

1. Debt Mutual Funds (Positive): Bond prices rate cut se upar jaate hain. Aapke Long Duration Debt fund mein roughly 1.2–2.5% immediate NAV gain expect karo. Rs.1L investment pe Rs.1,200–2,500 ek din mein.

2. Equity (Positive, moderate): Companies ka borrowing cost ghata → margin expansion → stock prices typically 2-5% upar jaate hain 30 din mein. IT aur NBFC sector ko sabse zyada fayda.

3. FD (Negative): Banks FD rates ghatate hain rate cut ke baad. Agar aapke paas existing FD hai toh lock-in karo. Naye FD mat karo abhi.

Aapke portfolio ke liye suggestion: Agar Long Duration Debt fund hai toh hold karo — rate cut cycle mein value badhegi. Short Duration ke liye consider karo switch to Medium Duration.
India vs China economy comparison — which country is better for investing?
NXANCELM — COUNTRY COMPARISON Short answer: India for next 10-20 years. China for short-term stability but structural risks.

Key Numbers (2024-25):
• GDP Growth: India 6.8% vs China 4.8% — India growing faster
• GDP Size: China $18T vs India $3.9T — China 4.5x bigger
• Debt/GDP: India 86% vs China 288% — China very high, systemic risk
• Demographics: India median age 28 vs China 40 — India has 20yr demographic advantage
• FDI: India growing, China declining as companies diversify away

India risks: Infrastructure deficit, slow judicial system, fiscal deficit, rural income gap
China risks: Property sector collapse (Evergrande), demographic decline, geopolitical isolation, debt bubble

For Indian investors: You cannot invest in China directly. But Mirae Asset China Fund (AMFI: 118551) and several International MFs have China exposure if you want diversification.
Best upcoming IPO kaun sa hai is quarter mein?
NXANCELM — IPO INTELLIGENCE IPO analysis based on DRHP filings and current GMP:

NxanceLM scores each IPO on 6 factors: Business quality, Valuation vs peers, Promoter background, Financial health (3yr), GMP trend, Anchor investor quality.

Analysis framework for any IPO:
• P/E vs Peers: If IPO P/E is 40x but listed peers trade at 25x → overvalued, skip
• GMP (Grey Market Premium): High GMP = listing gain likely, not investment quality
• Revenue growth 3yr: Must be above 20%/yr for growth sector IPOs
• Promoter shareholding post-IPO: Above 50% = aligned interests
• Use of proceeds: Expansion vs OFS (Original shareholders cashing out) — OFS is red flag

Rule from NxanceLM: GMP-based IPO investing ≠ portfolio investing. IPO is Day 1 bet. Portfolio is 10-year strategy. Nxance focuses on portfolio quality, not IPO listing gains.

Step 3 — Format and Output Rules
Rs. + % AlwaysNever write just "3.3% gap". Always write "3.3% gap = Rs.16,500/year on your Rs.5L".
3-Line Summary FirstFirst 3 lines = complete picture. User reads further if they want detail.
No Jargon AloneNever "CVaR". Always "CVaR (average loss on your worst days)". Always inline.
Source Every NumberEvery % has a source: (AMFI data), (NSE 20yr), (RBI report Dec 2025).
Hinglish DetectionIf user writes in Hindi/Hinglish, output is in Hinglish. Auto-detected.
SEBI DisclaimerAuto-appended when output could be construed as investment advice.
What NxanceLM Will NOT Do
No "Buy Now"Never says "Buy TCS today". Says "TCS fundamentals are strong. Here is the data. You decide."
No Wrong CodesNever invents AMFI codes. If unsure, says "verify on amfiindia.com".
No Outdated DataAlways fetches live data for questions about current rates, prices, or NAVs.
No OverconfidenceNever says "this will happen". Says "based on historical patterns, this has happened X% of the time".
No Personalized AdviceNever says "you should do X". Says "people in your situation often consider X — here is why". SEBI RIA boundary respected.

NxanceLM's Core Job: Every quantitative engine in Nxance produces numbers. NxanceLM converts those numbers into sentences a user actually understands. The Selection Engine says "FIT SCORE 91". NxanceLM says "We chose this fund because it has beaten its peers in 8 of the last 10 years and charges 6x less than what you currently pay". That is the translation job.
SEBI Boundary: NxanceLM is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser. It gives analysis, context, and education. It never says "buy this today". All outputs include a disclaimer when appropriate. The user always makes the final decision.
