THE SOLUTION
One app, three engines: Health Check, Construction, and NxanceLM — AI that explains why.
— Health Check: 26 sub-engines diagnose existing holdings (XIRR, VaR/CVaR, Sharpe/Sortino, Monte Carlo, FinBERT sentiment) into a 0–100 score
— Construction: builds an optimized portfolio (Black-Litterman + Ledoit-Wolf) from goals & risk profile
— NxanceLM: AI + live market terminal explaining every output in plain English/Hindi, backed by an evidence trail
